## Position and boundary

The site should help property owners, architects, contractors, facility teams, and buyers define, compare, procure, inspect, and maintain architectural-glass work. It owns service selection, system interfaces, survey/fabrication coordination, installation quality, and care for the evidenced service families.

It does not provide structural calculations, facade engineering sign-off, glass processing recipes, worker safety procedures, universal thickness/span tables, permit interpretations, or unsupported price/performance claims. Final glass make-up, dimensions, supports, anchors, holes/notches, wind/impact loads, deflection, drainage, accessibility, and fire performance require current standards and competent project-specific design.

## Existing-corpus and internal-link rules

1. Existing category/service hubs retain commercial intent. New articles support them and avoid exact-match hub slugs.
2. `jasa-pasang-{service}-{city}` pages must not seed new location variants. Repeated explanatory content should consolidate into the relevant KTG topic.
3. KTG-01 routes readers to system owners KTG-03 through KTG-09 only after defining the use and risk.
4. Every system topic links back to KTG-02 for glass make-up and forward to KTG-10 through KTG-12 for delivery.
5. KTG-13 owns all care and damage-triage intent; system articles mention maintenance only as a link.
6. KTG-14 summarizes deliverables and links to technical owners instead of repeating their criteria.
7. Tempered glass is a material/process choice, not a standalone answer to all safety, support, or post-breakage requirements.

## Evidence gates

- Glass make-up, thickness, dimensions, edgework, holes/notches, heat treatment, interlayers, coatings, support, anchor/fitting capacity, wind/impact loads, deflection, and post-breakage behaviour require current standards, traceable product/fabricator data, and competent project design.
- Curtain-wall air/water/structural/thermal/acoustic/fire claims require a named system, tested assembly, project details, and the applicable test report; material names alone do not prove performance.
- Overhead and guard glazing content must explicitly require risk assessment, redundancy/post-breakage design, and responsible engineering.
- Hardware grade, cycles, loads, corrosion class, sealant compatibility, and warranty require manufacturer documentation and actual exposure conditions.
- Prices, quantities, installation rates, lead times, maintenance intervals, service life, and guarantees require dated, like-for-like project evidence.
- Samples, mockups, certificates, site tests, photographs, and project references require traceability and permission.
- Accessibility, facade safety, fire, permits, public-use, and wet-area rules must be checked against current project jurisdiction and owner requirements.
- Chipped, cracked, moving, delaminating, leaking, or overhead glass must be isolated and assessed by competent parties; do not publish DIY drilling, cutting, strengthening, or structural repair instructions.

## First bounded cluster

Publish KTG-01, KTG-02, KTG-10, KTG-11, KTG-12, and KTG-14 first. This creates a safe selection-to-handover path before expanding system-specific guidance that depends on stronger test reports and project records.
